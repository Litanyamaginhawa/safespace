A Message for You 💙

Hello hello, Kumusta?


I don't want to interfere sa kung ano man ang pinagdadaanan mo right now, pero kanina ko pa kasi napapansin yung notes mo here sa IG. I just wanted to ask if okay ka lang? I really hope you're doing well and okay ka lang today.


I know hindi tayo masyadong magkakilala at IG moots lang tayo, pero gusto ko lang sabihin na valid kung ano man ang nararamdaman mo. Whenever things feel too heavy, please remember the pushcart. If the load is too much, it is completely okay to let the pushcart go for a moment. You can rest for a while, regain your strength, and then push it again when you are ready. You don't have to carry everything all at once. Just take things one step at a time. You have the strength to get through any challenges, so please keep going and stay positive.


Kaya mo 'yan, keep going! Take it one day at a time, okay? 🌸✨
